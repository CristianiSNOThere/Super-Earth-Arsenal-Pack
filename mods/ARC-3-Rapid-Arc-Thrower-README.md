# ARC-3 Rapid Arc Thrower

## Current gameplay changes

Sets minimum/full/overcharge times to 0.20/0.22/0.24 seconds, primary reach to 40 m, and normal/durable damage per pulse to 163/65. Sets Stun Medium buildup to 0.8, demolition strength to 4, hit reaction force strength to 25, impulse to 2, and horizontal/vertical camera climb multipliers to 0.4.

## Controls and compatibility

Enable Arc Thrower Revamped in CowboyBingus's Vanilla Plus Megapack for hold-to-fire.

## Install

Import the ZIP into Arsenal and enable its option. Keep Bingus Shared Loader at its documented priority, Purge and Deploy, and restart Helldivers 2. Choose the individual packages or the modpack to avoid duplicate addon resources.

Requires Helldivers 2 Steam build 25480438 and Bingus Shared Loader v18 or newer.
